# Changelog

Releases are the tags `chat-dock/vX.Y.Z` of https://github.com/nfour/bb-plugins.

## 0.1.3 (2026-10-30)

- The dock button shows where a click sends the composer: a chevron pointing right, toward the panel, while the composer is under the timeline, and pointing left once it is docked.

## 0.1.2 (2026-10-29)

- With the right panel split into tiles, the composer docks under the bottom tile (the left one of a bottom row), not the first tile BB created.

## 0.1.1 (2026-10-29)

- Requires BB 0.48 or later.
- The dock button switches docking on and off. The other options moved to a **Chat dock** submenu in the thread's actions menu.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-29)

- First release.
