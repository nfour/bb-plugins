## What you get

Open Thinking opens the agent's thinking by itself. While an agent works, BB's "Thinking…" line shows its text as it streams and the "Thought" row it turns into arrives already open. A "Thought" row that comes on screen later, because you unfolded a "Worked for" row or a group such as "Ran 3 commands", opens too.

Two options, off by default, close thinking again: when a new thought arrives (only the newest on screen stays open), and when the turn finishes (thinking closes, and thinking in finished turns stays closed).

Your own clicks win. Thinking you open or close yourself is left as you set it until the page is reloaded.

## What it does not do

BB folds "Thought" rows into its work groups, and a folded group takes its thinking off the page. Open Thinking opens thinking that is on the page. It keeps the group holding the newest thought open while the turn works, and unfolds nothing else.

## Settings

Pick **Open thinking** in a thread's actions menu, or open the plugin's page in BB's settings: **Open thinking blocks**, **Close earlier thinking blocks when a new one arrives** and **Close thinking blocks when the turn finishes**, **Keep the latest thinking in view while the turn works** and **Scroll growing thinking to its end**. A change applies at once. The other options do nothing while opening is off, and with it off the plugin attaches nothing to the page.

## Cost

It adds no element and no colour. One observer on the page looks only at nodes that hold a thinking row or toggle, nothing runs while the tab is in the background, and no timeline data is read.
